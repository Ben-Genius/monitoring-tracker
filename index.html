Questions

Good to know

Can other companies on Pulse360 see our work?

No. Each organisation is kept separate in the database itself, not just hidden in the app, and every request is checked against who is signed in.

Will my site staff actually use it?

They don’t need to learn a board. My work shows each person only their own tasks with one button for the next step, sized for a thumb. Accepting a task is a single tap.

Does it work on slow connections?

Yes. Pages load only what they need, it can be installed on a phone’s home screen, and it opens even when the connection drops.

We already run several companies. Can they share one brief?

Yes. That is what Pulse360 is for: one brief across all of them, with a filter for each company when you want to look closer.

Can I try it without inviting anyone?

Yes. Start free with the sample project and you’ll see a full brief, the 7am email and My work before you invite a single person.
