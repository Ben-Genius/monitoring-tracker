1. Who we are

Commit is provided by [Legal company name], [registered address], Ghana (“we”, “us”). We are registered with the Data Protection Commission of Ghana as a data controller, registration number [DPC registration number].

This notice explains how we handle personal data under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) when you visit our website, create an account, or use Commit as part of a team.

2. Your organisation and us

Commit is used by organisations (for example, a group of companies) to track their projects. Two different roles apply:

  • Workspace content. The organisation that set up the workspace decides what is recorded about its projects and people: tasks, updates, comments, files and who is invited. For that content the organisation is the data controller and we process it on its behalf and on its instructions. Questions about it are best sent to your organisation’s Commit administrator first; we will help them answer.
  • Accounts, the website and analytics. For your login details, our website, service emails and usage analytics, we are the data controller.

6. Where it is stored

Some of our providers store or process data outside Ghana, as listed above. Where data leaves Ghana, we use providers that commit by contract to protection at least equivalent to Act 843, and we limit what is sent to what each provider needs.

7. How long we keep it

  • Accounts and workspace content: for as long as your organisation uses Commit. If an administrator deactivates you, your name stays on work you did so the organisation’s records remain accurate, but you can no longer sign in.
  • When an organisation leaves: we delete its workspace within 30 days of the account being closed, after giving it a chance to export its data. Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.
  • Usage analytics: no longer than 24 months.
  • Morning brief send records: no longer than 12 months.
  • Billing and legal records: as long as Ghanaian tax and company law requires.

8. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Each organisation’s data is separated in the database itself, and every request is checked against the signed-in person and their role.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form. Uploaded files are private and opened only through short-lived links.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the staff who need it.
  • The installable app caches only the app itself on your device, never your organisation’s data.

If personal data is accessed or acquired by someone without authority, we will notify the Data Protection Commission and the affected organisations without delay, and help them inform the people concerned.

9. Your rights

Under Act 843 you can:

  • Find out whether we hold personal data about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. You can change your name and phone number yourself in Settings.
  • Ask us to delete data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, including analytics.
  • Stop the morning brief email at any time from the link in the email or in Settings.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Commission (see section 14).

To use these rights, email [privacy contact email]. We will reply within 21 days and may need to confirm your identity first. Where the request concerns workspace content, we will pass it to your organisation and help them respond.

10. Cookies and browser storage

Commit does not use advertising cookies. It stores a few items in your browser:

  • Sign-in session (essential): keeps you signed in. Removed when you sign out.
  • Preferences (essential): your light or dark theme and the company you last viewed.
  • App cache (essential): the app’s own files, so it opens quickly and without a connection. It never contains your organisation’s data.
  • Analytics identifier (analytics): a random identifier set by PostHog so visits can be counted. Analytics record named actions and page views only. They do not record what you type, the text of what you click, or your screen.

You can clear these in your browser settings at any time. Clearing the sign-in session will sign you out.

11. Emails

We send only service emails: invitations, sign-up confirmation, password resets, and the 7am morning brief to people who oversee an organisation. The morning brief summarises your organisation’s workspace for you. Every brief has a link to stop it. We do not send marketing emails without your separate agreement.

12. Children

Commit is a workplace tool for adults. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
